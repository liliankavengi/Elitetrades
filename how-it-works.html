How EliteTrades Works

No hidden fees. No complicated rules. Here is everything you need to know about our contracts, payouts, deposits, and withdrawals — plainly explained.

📈 Contract Types

Rise Contract

You predict that the market price will be higher when the contract expires compared to when you entered it. If correct, you win the fixed payout percentage of your stake.

Fall Contract

You predict that the market price will be lower when the contract expires. If correct, you win the fixed payout. If wrong, you lose your stake.

Both contract types are fixed-odds. Your maximum loss is limited to your stake. You cannot lose more than you put in on a single contract.

🏦 Deposits & Withdrawals

💳 Deposit Methods

M-Pesa (instant), Airtel Money, bank card (Visa / Mastercard), and EFT bank transfer. All deposits are credited instantly.

🏧 Minimum Deposit

$5 USD (approximately KES 750). There is no maximum deposit limit. Funds are available for trading immediately upon deposit confirmation.

⚡ Withdrawal Speed

Withdrawal requests are processed within 24 hours. M-Pesa withdrawals typically reflect within minutes of approval.

🔄 Withdrawal Rules

No minimum withdrawal amount. Withdrawals are returned to the original deposit method. ID verification may be required for larger withdrawals.

🎯 How Winning & Losing Works

If Your Prediction Is Correct

Your stake is returned to you plus 35% profit. Example: $50 stake → you receive $67.50 ($50 stake + $17.50 profit). Scale is linear — $1 stake → +$0.35 profit.

If Your Prediction Is Wrong

You lose your stake for that contract. Your maximum loss is your stake — nothing more. No margin calls. No debt.

Equal Entry Price

If the market price at contract expiry equals the entry price exactly (a tie), your stake is refunded in full. You neither win nor lose.
